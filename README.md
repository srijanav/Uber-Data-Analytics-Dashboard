# 🚖 Uber Data Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an end-to-end analysis of Uber ride booking data, focusing on booking trends, revenue generation, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders understand business performance through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs), enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The report consists of **four interactive pages**:

### 🏠 Introduction
- Dashboard overview
- Navigation menu
- Project landing page

---

### 📈 Revenue & Booking Performance

Key Metrics
- Total Bookings
- Average Daily Bookings
- Total Revenue
- Completed Ride Rate

Visualizations
- Revenue by Vehicle Type
- Revenue by Payment Method
- Monthly Booking Trend
- Bookings by Time of Day
- Top Pickup Locations by Revenue

Business Insights
- Analyze revenue contribution across vehicle categories.
- Identify booking trends throughout the day.
- Track monthly booking performance.
- Compare payment preferences.
- Discover high-demand pickup locations.

---

### ⚠️ Cancellation & Operational Analysis

Key Metrics
- Customer-Initiated Cancellations
- Driver-Initiated Cancellations
- Cancellation Rate
- Driver Cancellation Rate

Visualizations
- Customer Cancellation Reasons
- Driver Cancellation Reasons
- Cancellations by Vehicle Type
- Cancellation Rate by Pickup Location

Business Insights
- Identify major cancellation drivers.
- Compare customer and driver cancellation behavior.
- Evaluate cancellation rates across vehicle types.
- Detect operational hotspots based on pickup locations.

---

### ⭐ Customer Experience & Service Quality

Key Metrics
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Driver Rating
- Average Vehicle Arrival Time
- High Customer Rating Rate

Visualizations
- Customer Rating Distribution
- Driver Rating Distribution
- Average Customer Rating by Time of Day
- Customer Ratings by Pickup Location

Business Insights
- Monitor overall customer satisfaction.
- Analyze driver performance.
- Understand rating trends throughout the day.
- Identify locations delivering the best customer experience.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue is primarily driven by popular vehicle categories.
- Booking demand varies significantly across different times of the day.
- Driver and customer cancellations reveal distinct operational challenges.
- Customer satisfaction remains consistently high across completed rides.
- Certain pickup locations generate both higher revenue and higher booking volumes.
